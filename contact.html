Get in Touch

We're happy to hear from prospective students, collaborators, and visitors interested in computational imaging and physics-based vision. Reach out below, or stop by our office in the Computer Science building at Stony Brook.

We are hiring at all levels: interns, visiting students, PhDs, and postdocs. Please read if you are interested.

Prospective PhD students: Apply through the CS graduate admissions and mention Akshat Dave in your Statement of Purpose. Please also fill out this interest form.

Prospective postdocs and interns: Fill out the Google form and email dave@cs.stonybrook.edu with your CV (subject: "Prospective Postdoc" or "Prospective Intern").

Current SBU students: Fill out the Google form and email dave@cs.stonybrook.edu with your CV (subject: "SBU Student").
