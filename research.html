What is Photon Intelligence?

A machine's ability to capture, model, and infer the physics of light,
revealing what's invisible to us. 

Building blocks of Photon Intelligence

01

Capture

Computational imaging

Cameras that measure what ordinary sensors discard: the arrival time of individual photons, the polarization state of light, thermal emission, per-pixel change events. These extra dimensions encode scene information no RGB image contains.

Cameras we build & use
Time of Flight Single Photon Imaging Polarization Event Cameras Thermal Imaging Radar
02

Model

Light transport

Differentiable, physics-based models of how light moves through the world: bouncing between surfaces, scattering through volumes, reflecting off materials. Simulation that runs forward to predict measurements, and backward to explain them.

Light effects we model
Multi Bounce Scattering Surface Reflectance Volumetric Scattering Specular Reflections Birefringence Shadows
03

Infer

Physics-based machine learning

Learning algorithms that fuse captured signals with transport models to recover what neither could alone. Neural representations, deep priors, and test-time optimization turn ill-posed inverse problems into solvable ones.

Learning methods we develop
Inverse Problems Neural Rendering Implicit Representations Deep Priors Test Time Optimization Diffusion Models 3D Transformers

Outcome: Seeing the invisible

AR / VR

Digitizing objects and scenes so they stay faithful under any viewpoint and illumination, creating photorealistic, relightable assets for immersive worlds.

Robotics

Perception that holds up where cameras fail: seeing around corners, through glare, and in photon-starved conditions.

Biomedical imaging

Peering into scattering tissue at low light doses, pairing photon-limited measurements with learned priors for low-dose, depth-resolved modalities.

Quantum sensing

Photon-resolved measurement for precision metrology: time-tagging for ranging and polarization for structural contrast at the limits of resolution.
